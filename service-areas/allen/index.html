Emergency restoration for Allen homes and businesses

Allen is a consistent, largely post-1990 suburban build-out with a high owner-occupancy rate and a lot of two-story family homes. From our shop at 4828 Topline Drive in Dallas, Allen is up US-75 — which means when you call at 2am, the delay is drive time rather than a call center forwarding your details to a crew somewhere else.

We cover the whole city, including Twin Creeks, Bethany Lakes, Watters Crossing, Star Creek and Cottonwood Bend, and the areas around Watters Creek, the Allen Event Center, Celebration Park and the Allen Outlets. Common ZIP codes we work in: 75002, 75013.

What makes Allen properties different

Predominantly 1990s and 2000s construction: two-story, post-tension slab, PEX or CPVC supply, attic air handlers and frequently an upstairs laundry room. Well-built stock overall, with failures skewing toward appliances, water heaters and HVAC rather than pipe age.

What that means for you: Second-floor washing machine and water heater failures, attic condensate line clogs, and spring hail across Collin County. We take that into account before we arrive, which is part of why local dispatch matters more in this trade than in most.

What to do right now

If water is running, shut it off at the fixture or the main. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out of that area and let us handle the power. If there has been a fire, do not enter the structure until the fire department clears it. Then photograph everything before anything is moved, and call — the clock on secondary damage starts immediately, and mold can begin growing on damp materials within 24 to 48 hours.
