What this work involves

Workstation power is the recurring theme. Open-plan layouts rely on floor boxes, poke-throughs or furniture-integrated power, and each reconfiguration tends to leave power in the wrong place. Adding circuits during a reconfiguration is far cheaper than adding them afterwards.

Lighting is the second theme and the one occupants notice. Older troffers with fluorescent lamps produce flat, tiring light. LED retrofits improve both the quality and the operating cost, and adding occupancy sensing in meeting rooms and back-of-house areas reduces consumption further.

Why this is not a DIY job

Office electrical work is judged on whether the space works on Monday morning. Getting power and data to where the furniture actually goes is the whole job.

Safety notes

  • Extension cords and power strips daisy-chained under desks are a common and genuine finding.
  • Panel access must stay clear of storage and furniture.
