Recessed Lighting Installation in Addison, TX

Recessed lighting disappears into the ceiling, which is exactly the point. It is also the type of lighting most often installed badly, because a grid of evenly spaced cans is easy to specify and produces a flat, unflattering result.

What this work involves

Modern LED units have changed the calculation. Slim canless fixtures fit into shallow ceilings, produce far less heat, and many offer selectable colour temperature at the fixture. Retrofit units drop into existing housings and improve both light quality and the ceiling seal.

Placement responds to the room. Lighting a work surface means fixtures over the surface, not centred in the room. Washing a wall means placing them closer to it. General ambience means spacing that accounts for beam angle and ceiling height. These are different layouts, and most rooms want a combination.

Things that affect the job

  • Joist positions and HVAC runs constrain placement
  • IC-rated housings are required in insulated ceilings
  • Ceiling height determines useful spacing
  • Air-sealed housings reduce conditioned-air loss into the attic

Residential

  • Kitchen and living area lighting
  • Hallway and stairwell lighting
  • Bathroom and shower-rated fixtures
  • Sloped ceiling installations
  • Retrofit of existing housings

Commercial

  • Office and reception lighting
  • Retail accent and display lighting

Why this is not a DIY job

Every recessed fixture is a hole in a ceiling that has to miss a joist, a duct and a cable run. Planning the layout around what is actually up there is why it is worth having someone look first.

Safety notes

  • Housings not rated for insulation contact can overheat when insulation is packed around them.
  • Fixtures over showers and tubs must be rated for damp or wet locations.

Benefits

  • Clean ceilings with no visual clutter
  • Even coverage without dark corners
  • Minimal heat compared with older housings
  • Dimmable with compatible controls
  • Selectable colour temperature on many modern units
