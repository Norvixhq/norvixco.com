Do not wait on these

Problems that should not be left

Most electrical work can be planned. A short list of symptoms indicates heat or a fault developing right now, and those warrant a call the same day.

Call(469) 425-8874
  • Burning smell from an outlet or panel
  • Breaker that trips repeatedly or will not reset
  • Scorch marks or discolouration on a device
  • Buzzing or crackling from a panel or switch
  • Outlets or cover plates warm to the touch
  • Lights across the house brightening and dimming
  • A shock from an appliance or fixture
  • Partial power loss with no obvious cause

If there is smoke, sparking or fire

Call 911 first. For a downed power line, stay well clear, keep others clear, and call 911 and the utility — a line on the ground can energise the ground around it.

Straight answers and work that lasts
