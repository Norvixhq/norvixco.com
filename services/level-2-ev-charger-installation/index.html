EV, Generator & High-Demand

Level 2 EV Charger Installation in Addison, TX

Level 2 is the standard for home charging because it matches the way people actually use a car: plug in at night, leave with a full battery. Getting there means a dedicated 240-volt circuit and an honest look at what the panel can carry.

What this work involves

The practical difference is stark. Level 1 on a standard outlet adds roughly three to five miles of range per hour. Level 2 typically adds five to nine times that, depending on the unit and the vehicle. For anyone driving normal daily distances, that turns charging from something to plan around into something that simply happens.

Amperage selection is where judgement comes in. The largest unit is not automatically the best one. A 40-amp charger on a 50-amp circuit fits comfortably in far more homes than a 48-amp unit needing a 60-amp circuit, and overnight the difference rarely matters. Matching the unit to the panel avoids turning a straightforward installation into a service upgrade.

Things that affect the job

  • Continuous load sizing: a 48-amp unit requires a 60-amp circuit
  • Vehicle onboard charger capacity may cap the useful rate
  • Panel capacity governs the practical maximum
  • Wi-Fi connected units need usable signal at the mounting point

Residential

  • Garage wall-mounted units
  • Exterior and carport installations
  • Hardwired and receptacle-based installations
  • Dual-vehicle setups

Commercial

  • Workplace and tenant parking charging

Why this is not a DIY job

Continuous loads are the category where sizing errors do not announce themselves. A conductor slightly undersized for a charger runs warm for hours every night for years.

Safety notes

  • The circuit must be dedicated. Sharing it with other loads undermines the sizing entirely.
  • Never use an extension cord or adapter with a Level 2 unit.

Benefits

  • Full overnight charging for typical daily use
  • A dedicated circuit isolated from other loads
  • Amperage matched to available panel capacity
  • Scheduling and monitoring features on connected units
